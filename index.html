    FAQ

    How do I see my charge? Enter the number on your blue tag in the Home tab. Tags run from 1 to 100.

    My tag shows nothing. We write percentages in from time to time. Message us and we will check.

    I lost my tag. We cannot release your item without the original tag. Please keep your tag safe.

    Can I do POS here? Yes. The POS cash on the board shows what is available today.

    Security services
    • Every device gets a numbered tag at drop-off.
    • Items are released only to the person presenting the original claim tag.
    • Devices stay in our supervised charging area.
    Terms
    • Lost tags mean unclaimed items.
    • Due to repeated loss of claim tags, we no longer release any phone or property without the original tag issued at drop-off.
    • If you lose your tag, we cannot release your items. Please keep your tag safe.
