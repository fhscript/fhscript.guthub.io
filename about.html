About us

Abasieyene Oil and Gas is a leading petroleum products distributor based in Etinan, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria.

Who we are

Abasieyene Oil and Gas (operated by Abasieyene Ini Investment Limited) runs a commercial petrol filling station in Etinan. We distribute refined petroleum products and provide everyday services that keep our community moving.

With years of hands-on experience in the oil and gas sector, we focus on reliable supply, fair pricing and practical services for drivers, businesses and households.

Core business

⛽

Petroleum products

Premium Motor Spirit (PMS / petrol) and Automated Gas Oil (diesel) for cars, bikes, generators and commercial vehicles.

💳

POS services

Cash withdrawals and transfers at the station for customers who need convenient banking access.

🔌

Device charging

Secure charging for phones, power banks, laptops and more — drop off, get a tag, collect when ready.
